An organic farm and orchard, by season.

March to April

Spring flowers फूलदेई

The garden comes into flower. Help with the spring sowing, and join the village children for Phool Dei, when they leave flowers on every doorstep.

May to July

Mango season आम

Rausil is known for its mangoes. Pick them in the orchard, along with bananas and wild berries from the forest edge, and turn the surplus into chutney.

July to September

Harela and the green हरेला

At Harela, Kumaon's festival of green, everyone plants a tree. The ferns unroll, the fields fill, and the fireflies come out at night.

October to February

Spice and harvest तेजपत्ता

Dry the tej patta from our own trees, bring in the millet, make pickles, and sit in the winter sun on the lawn.

Welcome to The Dale

A few things we ask, with love.

  • Quiet hours. Music low, and silent after 10 pm. Events keep their own timings.

  • Spring water. Our water comes from a spring on the estate. Please use it gently.

  • All meals included. Tell us about any dietary needs when you book.

  • Pets welcome. Keep them with you, and off the beds.

  • The hall. Open to every guest: books, the pool table, the projector.

  • Power. We have backup, but the hills cut out sometimes.

Good to know

Are meals included?

Yes, all meals, cooked from our farm and orchard.

Will we get stuck in Kainchi Dham or Nainital traffic?

No. Our road leaves the highway at Ranibagh, at the foot of the hills.

Are the rooms air-conditioned?

Yes. Every room has AC, an attached washroom and a mini fridge.

Can we book the whole place?

Yes: ten rooms for up to 20 guests, with the lawn, the hall and the restaurant.

Can we bring our dog?

Yes. Tell us when you book.

Is there a Neem Karoli Baba ashram nearby?

Yes, about 500 m away in Rausil. Kainchi Dham is a longer drive; we can arrange an early car.
